# TicketBox Design Direction

## Visual Strategy
- Base theme: dark concert atmosphere
- Color strategy: restrained with a committed hero section
- Emphasis on large hierarchy and clear rhythms

## Palette (OKLCH)
- Ink 900: oklch(0.16 0.01 260)
- Ink 800: oklch(0.2 0.012 260)
- Ink 700: oklch(0.26 0.014 260)
- Fog 100: oklch(0.86 0.02 260)
- Ember 500 (primary): oklch(0.68 0.17 35)
- Ember 400 (hover): oklch(0.72 0.16 35)
- Cyan 400 (secondary): oklch(0.78 0.14 210)
- Success: oklch(0.73 0.15 145)
- Warning: oklch(0.79 0.15 85)
- Error: oklch(0.6 0.2 25)

## Typography
- Headings: Space Grotesk
- Body: IBM Plex Sans
- Scale ratio: 1.2
- Line length: 65-75ch for prose

## Layout
- Predictable grids for product screens
- Hero can be immersive and asymmetric
- Use varied spacing for rhythm

## Motion
- 180-240ms for most transitions
- Motion indicates state and focus
- Prefer opacity and transform changes

## Components
- Consistent button, input, badge, and tab vocabulary
- Full state coverage: hover, focus, active, disabled, loading

## Bans
- No gradient text
- No side stripe borders as accents
- Avoid identical card grids
- Avoid decorative glassmorphism
